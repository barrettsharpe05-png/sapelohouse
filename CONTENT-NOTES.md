# Sapelo House Content Notes

These items require owner-provided information before final launch.

- Exact street address or final map embed, if the owner wants a map on the Location page.
- Booking engine URL, email delivery endpoint, or static-site form handler for the inquiry form.
- Direct booking policies, rates, fees, availability rules, check-in/check-out times, cancellation terms, and payment process.
- Bedroom count, bathroom count, sleeping capacity, bed sizes, accessibility features, pet policy, and parking details.
- Final hero images if the owner wants different page-specific hero selections.
- Owner-approved contact email, phone number, and social links.
- Confirmation that `https://sapelohouse.com/` is the preferred canonical host, plus production redirects for HTTP, `www`, and duplicate URL variants.
- Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools verification access for sitemap submission and index monitoring.
- Analytics or consent-management IDs, if the owner wants traffic and conversion reporting.
- Production CDN or firewall confirmation that OAI-SearchBot, ChatGPT-User, Googlebot, and Bingbot can crawl without a bot challenge.
- Google Hotel Center eligibility and the required identifier, occupancy, full address, and coordinates before adding Google-specific `VacationRental` rich-result markup.
